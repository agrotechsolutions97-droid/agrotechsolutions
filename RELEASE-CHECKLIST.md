# Release checklist

- [ ] Final domain replaces every `YOUR-REAL-DOMAIN` placeholder.
- [ ] Privacy Policy placeholders are completed and reviewed.
- [ ] Tally URL is configured and a real test enquiry is delivered.
- [ ] Cloudflare account, registrar, form provider, and business email use two-factor authentication.
- [ ] HTTPS is active and DNSSEC is enabled after DNS validation.
- [ ] All navigation, WhatsApp, phone, email, images, sitemap, robots, and 404 page are tested live.
- [ ] No secrets, passwords, recovery codes, customer data, or test submissions are present in the deployed files.
- [ ] Cloudflare Pages rollback history is understood by the business owner.
